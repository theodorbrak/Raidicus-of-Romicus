# Raidicus-of-Romicus
Raid of rome by the forces of ratticus, defend thyself in the year 1527, accidentally named hackatime project as terra -_-


So this came to me when I heard the name Ratticus, I watch polandball on YT and they have a Italy ball that just ends every word with icus, such as Romicus (Rome), additionally I've been listening to The Last Stand by Sabaton which has me feeling all kinds of things :) 

It's a top down combat game, you can either go solo or get troops (WIP), defeat the forces and move closer to Ratticus, lose or retreat and you get reinforcements but get closer to endangering the pope... 
Planned for multiple endings

Play on the itch.io page, otherwise download files, put them in same folder and launch main.py in any editor
